About

I am a software engineer focused on creating systems that make difficult work easier. Professionally, I design AWS-native platforms that support biomedical research, clinical genomics, data processing, and machine learning. My work centers on reusable infrastructure, distributed workflows, developer enablement, and reliable cloud operations.

Outside of work, I produce, mix, and master music and build software inspired by limitations I encounter in creative tools. Across both areas, the common thread is craftsmanship: understanding a problem deeply, designing a thoughtful system, and continuing to improve it until it becomes genuinely useful.

Cross-account ECR repository construct
